# mediCetamol 🩺

**A simple PYQ-focused study platform for medical entrance preparation.**

## 🔗 Open mediCetamol

**Website:**  
https://medicetamol.github.io/medquiz/

## 📚 About this app

mediCetamol is made for medical students preparing for competitive postgraduate entrance examinations such as **NEET-PG, INI-CET and FMGE**.

The focus is simple:

- 📝 Practice **Previous Year Questions**
- 📖 Revise important concepts
- 🎯 Focus on weak subjects and topics
- 📊 Track your question-solving progress
- 🔖 Bookmark questions for later revision
- ⚡ Create customised question modules
- 🧠 Learn from explanations instead of simply memorising answers

The goal is to make PYQ practice **simple, fast and distraction-free**.

## 🩻 What you can do

### PYQs

Practice questions organised by **exam → subject → topic**.

### Custom Modules

Select the subjects and topics you want to practice and create your own question set.

### Progress

Keep track of your question-solving journey and identify subjects that need more attention.

### Bookmarks

Save important questions from anywhere in the app for quick revision later.

## 🏆 Built for the final stretch

Whether you're starting your preparation or doing your final revision, mediCetamol is designed around one thing:

> **Solve more. Revise smarter. Improve continuously.**
